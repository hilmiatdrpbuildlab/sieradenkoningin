# Brand Identity Guidelines: Sieradenkoningin

This document outlines the core brand identity elements for "Sieradenkoningin" based on the brand mood board. It serves as a foundational reference for web design, marketing materials, and product curation.

## 🔑 Brand Keywords
These keywords dictate the tone of voice, visual aesthetic, and overall customer experience. The brand balances high-end aesthetics with everyday wearability.

*   Elegant
*   Feminine
*   Luxurious
*   Timeless
*   Empowering
*   Modern
*   High quality
*   Accessible luxury
*   For every day
*   Met betekenis (With meaning)

## 🖋️ Typography
The typography combines classic elegance with modern readability and feminine accents. 

*   **Primary Heading Font: Playfair Display**
    *   *Vibe:* Elegant & Luxe.
    *   *Usage:* Main titles, logos, large promotional banners, and prominent quotes (e.g., "SIERADENKONINGIN", "MORE THAN JEWELRY").
*   **Body Font: Montserrat**
    *   *Vibe:* Clean & Modern.
    *   *Usage:* Paragraph text, product descriptions, navigation menus, and buttons. Its sans-serif nature ensures excellent readability on screens.
*   **Accent Font: Allura**
    *   *Vibe:* Feminine Touch.
    *   *Usage:* Subtle accents, signatures, or specific emotional keywords (e.g., the word "You" in the mood board). Use sparingly for maximum impact.

## ✨ Icon Elements
The brand utilizes delicate, line-art style icons that convey royalty and elegance. These should be rendered in the brand's accent colors (Warm Cognac or Dark Espresso).

*   **The Crown:** The primary brand emblem, representing the "Koningin" (Queen) aspect of the name. It features a classic, intricate design with small jewel-like details on the tips.
*   **The Clover:** A four-leaf clover motif featuring a distinct, vintage-inspired shape. Used heavily in the jewelry designs and as a supporting graphic element.
*   **The Sparkle/Star:** A minimalist, four-pointed sparkle icon used to denote luxury, shine, or special features.

## 🛍️ Product Categories
The shop's taxonomy is clearly defined, with custom minimalist icons used for navigation. 

*   **Ringen** (Rings)
*   **Armbanden** (Bracelets)
*   **Kettingen** (Necklaces)
*   **Oorbellen** (Earrings)
*   **Sets** (Jewelry Sets)
*   **Accessoires** (Accessories - e.g., keychains/sleutelhangers, phone charms/telefooncharms)

## 💎 Product & Material Inspiration
The jewelry designs heavily influence the visual weight of the website. 

*   **Metals:** Warm, rich yellow gold. Textures include smooth, polished finishes as well as textured, braided, or "rope" style bands.
*   **Stones & Inlays:** Deep, rich burgundy/red stones (resembling garnet, dark ruby, or red agate) cut in square or classic clover shapes.
*   **Style:** Layered, stackable pieces. The aesthetic is confident and bold yet delicate enough for daily wear.

## 💻 Website Feel
The digital storefront should feel like stepping into a high-end, moody boutique.

*   **Aesthetic:** Dark, moody luxury. 
*   **Imagery:** High-contrast, warm-toned photography featuring confident models wearing stacked jewelry. Close-ups on hands, necks, and ears to highlight product details against dark red/burgundy fabrics.
*   **Layout:** Clean, editorial-style grids. 
*   **UI Elements:** Minimalist "Shop Now" buttons with thin borders or solid dark fills. The interface should not distract from the rich imagery but rather frame it elegantly.
